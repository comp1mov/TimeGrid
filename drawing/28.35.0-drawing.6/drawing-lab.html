What to test

Start in Single. Hold the brush while playing: every displayed frame receives a mark, even if your hand stops. Long Stroke carries the previous path forward; Trail 0 keeps all of it, a positive Trail limits the carried segments. Releasing keeps the recorded frames. One Undo removes the whole gesture.

1F clips the brush to the cell under the pointer, including its edge. Grid and Single are views of the same frame drawings. Grid playback changes the frame in each cell using TimeGrid's timing. FRM follows frame identity when columns, pattern or timing change. TPL repeats across frames. Apply TPL copies its appearance onto all frames and clears TPL, with one Undo. Onion Skin is a preview guide only.

BG has separate Grid and Single surfaces. New BG strokes can extend outside the composition. Export BG includes the full exterior drawing; disable it to use the normal composition bounds. Mouse/trackpad drag draws. Wheel or two fingers zoom. Right/middle drag pans. Space plays or pauses. Touch draws with one finger; two fingers navigate. Turn off Draw with finger for pen-only drawing. Pen pressure takes priority over speed. Hardware eraser uses the selected brush. Shift constrains a segment to 45° directions.

Save stores stroke geometry and layout/timing settings, with schema 5. Older lab drawings retain their saved geometry. Images loaded with Preview image sequence are temporary session references, ordered by filename, repeating across 96 slots; they are not saved in the drawing file. PNG uses the current frame mapping and layers, rerendered from geometry. This is not yet a full media project or video proxy workflow. Fill is the next separate step.

The drawing tile cache is capped at 24 MiB. Soft-brush sprites add at most 4 MiB. Empty frames allocate no drawing tiles. Undo is session-only, up to 100 gestures with a 16 MiB geometry estimate budget; the latest gesture is retained. Save before closing. Physical touchscreen, iPad/Pencil and Wacom testing remains necessary. Benchmark measures a separate temporary document and downloads a report.
